READING THIS RESULT -- the most important lesson in the project:
  These rules PASS. The statistics are not broken; the dependence is
  genuinely there. But it comes from SORTING and from FEATURE LEAKAGE,
  not from the lottery. A null model tests whether structure exists.
  It cannot tell you the structure is an artefact of your own
  preprocessing. Only reading the rules can do that.
  Statistical significance is necessary, not sufficient.
